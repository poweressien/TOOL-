About {{ SITE_NAME }}

{{ SITE_NAME }} researches phones, e-mails, domains, URLs, usernames, IPs, numbers and names using public sources only. Every claim lists its sources and a confidence score, and the exact searches that were run are shown on each result.

What it checks

  • Several search engines (DuckDuckGo built in; SerpAPI, Google CSE and Brave with your own keys), merged, de-duplicated and scored.
  • Deep mode: follow-up (pivot) searches, page verification, official-site probing, certificate transparency, archive history, look-alike domains.
  • News, Wikipedia, Wikidata, GitHub, Hacker News, Gravatar (if public), 30-platform handle presence, TLS, sitemap/robots/security.txt.
  • Phone parsing (carrier, region, type), DNS/mail posture, WHOIS/RDAP, IP network registration.

What it will not do

  • No private, leaked or data-broker records; people-search sites are excluded from identity inference.
  • No home-address or personal-contact harvesting. Only organisations' own published contacts are shown.
  • It cannot say who owns a private phone number or mailbox. If nothing public exists, it says so.
